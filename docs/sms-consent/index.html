How SMS Opt-In Works

SMS consent is collected during account onboarding at xoori.ai, immediately after you sign up. You may optionally enter your phone number and check a standalone, unchecked consent box that reads:

"I agree to receive personalized size recommendations and one follow-up fit question per recommendation via SMS from Xoori at the phone number I provided. Standard message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your requests. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP anytime to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for support."

You will receive only the following message types: (a) a one-time confirmation message when you opt in, (b) personalized size recommendations sent in direct response to product URLs you text us, (c) if we cannot read the retailer's published size chart for an item you sent us, one reply asking you to upload it, with a link, and (d) one follow-up fit question per recommendation so we can improve accuracy.
