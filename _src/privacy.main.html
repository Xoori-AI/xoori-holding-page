Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Xoori ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit xoori.ai and use our services, including our SMS fit recommendation feature.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information (email, name) when you sign up
  • Body measurements you enter for fit predictions
  • Phone number (if you opt in to SMS recommendations)
  • Search queries and style preferences
  • Images you upload for visual search
  • Photos you upload of a retailer's size chart or a garment's brand tag. We read the measurements printed on them and keep those measurements. The photo itself is kept for up to 30 days so we can check the reading was correct, and is then deleted automatically.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time on page
  • Referral source

3. SMS Communications

If you opt in to SMS size recommendations, the following applies:

  • You will receive only the following message types: (a) a one-time confirmation message when you opt in, (b) personalized size recommendations sent in direct response to product URLs you text us, (c) if we cannot read the retailer's published size chart for an item you sent us, one reply asking you to upload it, with a link, and (d) one follow-up fit question per recommendation so we can improve accuracy. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your requests.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for support, or email help@xoori.ai.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, sold, rented, or transferred to any third parties.
  • SMS opt-in consent is collected via a standalone, unchecked consent checkbox during account onboarding at xoori.ai, immediately after you create an account. Providing a phone number is optional and consent is not a condition of using Xoori. Program details: xoori.ai/sms-consent.
  • Xoori SMS number: +1 (844) 976-4082

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our fashion search and fit prediction services
  • To send personalized size recommendations via SMS (if opted in)
  • To personalize your shopping experience
  • To manage your account and preferences
  • To communicate with you about our services
  • To comply with legal obligations

5. AI and Image Processing

When you use our visual search feature, we process your uploaded images using third-party AI services (Anthropic Claude). These images are:

  • Processed temporarily to identify fashion items
  • Not permanently stored by us or our AI providers
  • Deleted immediately after processing
  • Never used to train AI models without your explicit consent

6. Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: AI providers, database hosting, SMS delivery (Twilio)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

The above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

7. Data Security

  • Encrypted database connections (PostgreSQL)
  • Secure API communication (HTTPS)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of SMS communications (reply STOP)
  • Export your data

Contact us: privacy@xoori.ai

We use cookies to enhance your experience and analyze usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
